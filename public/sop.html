Why This Program?

My interest in electronics has gradually developed into a strong desire to understand how modern electronic devices and semiconductor technologies work. Through my school project on piezoelectric energy generation and my experience assembling a personal computer, I have become more interested in the science and engineering behind the technology we use every day. I want to study a programme that gives me a strong foundation in electrical and electronic engineering, semiconductor devices, microelectronics and related fields. I am particularly interested in understanding how transistors and integrated circuits work, how chips are designed, and how semiconductor devices are manufactured. I believe that studying this field at the undergraduate level will help me connect the concepts I learn in Physics and Mathematics with practical engineering applications. It will also give me opportunities to develop my technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities and hands-on skills.

My fascination with semiconductor engineering began at my workbench while researching and assembling a custom personal computer from scratch. As I compared DRAM timings, die architectures, and processor nodes to source compatible components, my curiosity shifted from how a computer performs at the system level to how its core components operate at the atomic scale. Holding a central processing unit before seating it into the motherboard socket, I was captivated by a fundamental question: how can billions of microscopic gates be etched onto a fingernail sized piece of silicon with near zero defect? Investigating this question led me to the foundational principles of photolithography and silicon printing. Learning how tiny tin droplets with the help of CO2 lasers to print nanoscale integrated circuits transformed me from a hardware enthusiast into an aspiring semiconductor engineer.

What I Hope to Achieve?

During my undergraduate studies, I hope to build a strong understanding of electronics, semiconductor physics, circuit design and microelectronics. I want to learn not only the theoretical concepts but also how they are applied in real engineering situations.

I would like to gain practical experience through laboratory work, projects, internships and interaction with faculty members and fellow students. I also hope to improve my programming and analytical skills, as I understand that they are increasingly important in modern electronics and chip development.

Most importantly, I want to become a more independent learner, develop the confidence to work on challenging technical problems and gradually discover the areas of semiconductor engineering in which I can make the greatest contribution.

Long-Term Goals

My long-term goal is to build a career in the semiconductor and electronics industry. I am particularly interested in areas such as VLSI design, semiconductor devices, microelectronics and chip fabrication.

After completing my undergraduate degree, I hope to gain further knowledge through advanced studies or practical industry experience. I would like to work on real engineering challenges and contribute to the development of more efficient and reliable electronic technologies.

I understand that achieving this goal will require continuous learning, patience and dedication. I am prepared to build my knowledge step by step, learn from my experiences and keep improving my technical abilities. Ultimately, I hope to contribute meaningfully to the field of semiconductor technology.
