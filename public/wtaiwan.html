Pathway to the Silicon Island 🇮🇳 ➡️ 🇹🇼

I do not just want to study engineering, I want to study it where the future of global technology is physically manufactured. Taiwan's sillicon fabricators and its ecosystem of world-leading foundries like TSMC represent the absolute pinnacle of semiconductor fabrication. Advanced NVIDIA, AMD, Apple & MediaTek chips are manufactured in Taiwan's Fabs. I want to work on ASML's Extreme Ultraviolet lithography machines and see manufacturing process firsthand. My academic roadmap is entirely focused on entering an elite undergraduate program in 2027, adapting to the rigorous, high-tech academic culture, and eventually contributing to microelectronics research landscape through a Master's and Ph.D. track. And the only place where I can achieve this is in Taiwan.
