Why This Program?

My fascination with semiconductor engineering began at my workbench while researching and assembling a custom personal computer from scratch just before the global memory shortage. As I compared DRAM timings, die architectures, and processor nodes to source compatible components, my curiosity shifted from how a computer performs at the system level to how its core components operate at the atomic scale. Holding a central processing unit before seating it into the motherboard socket, I was captivated by a fundamental question: how can billions of microscopic gates be etched onto a fingernail sized piece of silicon with near zero defect? Investigating this question led me to the foundational principles of photolithography and silicon printing. Learning how tiny tin droplets with the help of CO2 lasers to print nanoscale integrated circuits transformed me from a hardware enthusiast into an aspiring semiconductor engineer. To turn this curiosity into technical mastery, I am applying to the Global Undergraduate Program in Semiconductors(GUPS) at National Taiwan University(NTU), an institution situated at the very epicenter of the global semiconductor supply chain

Long-Term Goals

My long-term roadmap is based in advancing next-generation semiconductor Research and Development (R&D). Immediately upon completing my undergraduate degree at NTU, I plan to pursue a Master's degree and potentially a Ph.D. focusing on advanced lithography scaling and energy-efficient integrated circuit architecture. Following my graduate studies, I aim to join the R&D division of a global semiconductor pioneers such as NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel, all of which collaborate deeply with Taiwan's fabrication ecosystem to push the boundaries of high-performance computing and AI hardware. Working within a world-class industrial R&D team will allow me to contribute to commercial breakthroughs in sub-nanometer chip design and advanced packaging, while building the deep technical and operational expertise required to eventually compliment my own deep-tech hardware innovations in the future. NTU's GUPS is the definitive launchpad for this journey and I am ready to contribute my curiosity, work ethic, and cross-disciplinary skill set to your academic community.
